That's the opposite of this system's own design principle, visible right in the eval trace: "status=needs_evidence — the worker's 'done' is an opinion. Only the gate records completion." A crash between dispatch and result-recording is precisely a case where the true outcome is unknown — it could have succeeded, failed, or partially applied with side effects. Silently defaulting to 'ok' lets an unverified action masquerade as a verified one, potentially advancing run state (and eventually gate/complete) on a false premise, which is the exact failure mode the gate+receipt machinery exists to prevent. Safer defaults would be to mark it 'unknown'/'failed' and force explicit reconciliation (re-run the check, inspect side effects, or require an operator/gate decision) before resuming forward.
